Postdoctoral researcher, KU Leuven

Joshua M. Sims

Computational and theoretical chemistry

Joshua Sims is a postdoc in Jeremy Harvey's group at KU Leuven. He mostly works on catalysis, on surfaces and in solution: what a catalyst looks like under reaction conditions, and how fast it turns over.

He also works on surface science, photochemistry and chirality-induced spin selectivity.

News

  • Oct 2026

    Perrhenate-catalysed deoxydehydration, in Chemical Science

    Open-access paper with Jeremy Harvey, accepted in Chemical Science. An agent-assisted reaction-network search with microkinetic modelling locates where the proton acts in perrhenate-catalysed deoxydehydration: it converts Re–OH to water at the water-loss steps, which raises the predicted rate by eight orders of magnitude over proton-free chemistry. 10.1039/D6SC06865K
  • Oct 2026

    Carbodicarbene monolayers on gold, in Small

    Open-access paper from Elad Gross’s group on carbodicarbene (CDC) monolayers on Au(111): a carbon(0) centre that binds strongly to the surface and lowers its work function by up to 1 eV. Sims contributed to the DFT calculations, with Carine Michel. 10.1002/smll.76364
  • Oct 2026

    New preprint: spin-aware reaction-network exploration in AutoMeKin

    Sole-author paper describing the spin-aware layer for AutoMeKin: automatic minimum-energy crossing points, nonadiabatic hop rates and an ⟨S2⟩ check on the single-reference picture, tested on three iron systems. 10.26434/chemrxiv.15010937/v1
  • Oct 2026

    New preprint: adsorption free energy of a mobile molecular catalyst

    Sole-author paper on fac-Re(bpy)(CO)3Cl on graphene. Metadynamics gives −64.1 kJ mol−1 where the harmonic approximation gives −26.3, and almost all of the gap comes from the complex moving and turning on the surface, not from anharmonicity. 10.26434/chemrxiv.15010935/v1
  • Oct 2026

    New preprint: when can an off-cycle reservoir raise a catalyst’s TON?

    Sole-author kinetics paper. A dormant reservoir only raises the turnover number when deactivation is of higher order in catalyst than turnover, and a dilution series shows in advance whether it will. Every number in it comes from tonkit. 10.26434/chemrxiv.15010890/v1
  • Oct 2026

    New preprint: O-selective radical etherification

    With Upendra Sharma and Erik Van der Eycken's groups: a visible-light, triphenylphosphine-mediated route to sterically congested ethers from 1,2-diketones. Sims contributed the computational study of the mechanism. 10.26434/chemrxiv.15006666/v2
  • Oct 2026
