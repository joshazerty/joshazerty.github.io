Postdoctoral researcher, KU Leuven

Computational and theoretical chemistry

I am a postdoc in Jeremy Harvey's group at KU Leuven. Catalysis is where most of my work sits, both on surfaces and in solution.

The rest spreads across computational and theoretical chemistry: reaction mechanisms, photochemistry and excited states, spin and chirality, molecules on surfaces, and machine-learned potentials.

  • Oct 2025

    Started at KU Leuven

    Joined the group of Prof. Jeremy Harvey as a postdoctoral researcher, working on mechanism and kinetics in computational catalysis, with computational work in the iBOF consortium on deoxydehydration.
  • Jul 2025

    PhD defended at the ENS de Lyon

    Defended unconditionally on 17 July. Thesis: morphology and surface state of a Ru/TiO2 catalyst, supervised by Dr Carine Michel.
  • 2025

    Oxygen vacancies paper out in PCCP

    First-author study of how support defects reshape sub-nanometre Ru clusters on titania, with a preprint on ChemRxiv. 10.1039/D5CP01579K
  • 2025

    Hexafluoroacetone on ice, in J. Phys. Chem. C

    Joint work with Javier Giorgi's group in Ottawa, pairing surface-science measurements with calculations of the hydrated form. 10.1021/acs.jpcc.5c00560
  • 2024

    Two talks on Ru nanoparticle morphology

    Oral presentations at ICTAC in Seville and at the Rencontres Théomsia-RCTF, plus a poster at the 18th International Congress on Catalysis in Lyon.
  • 2024

    Carbene monolayers on gold, in ACS AMI

    I did the computational half of an experimental study of how fluorinated N-heterocyclic carbene monolayers change Au surface properties. 10.1021/acsami.4c12514
