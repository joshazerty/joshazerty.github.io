Teaching

Courses

ENS de Lyon, 2022 to 2025. Sixty-four hours a year, taught in French and English.

  • L3

    Statistical physics tutorials

    Third-year undergraduate problem classes, 26 hours per year. Working through ensembles, partition functions and the statistical origin of thermodynamic quantities with small groups.
  • M1

    Periodic DFT practicals

    First-year master's laboratory sessions, 12 hours per year. Students run their first plane-wave calculations, meet convergence testing, and find out what a badly chosen k-point mesh does to an answer.
  • M2

    Computational chemistry practicals

    Second-year master's, 26 hours per year. Organisation of the practical programme, supervision of student project work, and the setting and marking of the theoretical chemistry examinations. Also tutoring for the agrégation preparation.
